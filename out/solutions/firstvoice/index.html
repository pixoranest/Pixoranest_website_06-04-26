About FirstVoice

The complete AI voice receptionist and answering service for modern businesses

FirstVoice is PixoraNest's flagship AI voice receptionist and 24/7 call answering service. It replaces missed calls, voicemail, IVR menus and expensive live answering services with a single intelligent voice agent platform that answers, understands, qualifies and routes every inbound business call — automatically.

Built as an enterprise-grade AI phone agent and virtual phone receptionist, FirstVoice combines voice AI software, conversation intelligence software, call routing software and deep CRM automation into one seamless customer communication layer. Whether you operate a startup, a growing SMB or a multi-location enterprise, FirstVoice acts as a tireless virtual receptionist software that delivers consistent, on-brand conversations at any scale.

What FirstVoice does

FirstVoice is engineered as an end-to-end automated answering service and digital receptionist software. The moment a call lands on your business number, the AI answering service greets the caller in their preferred language, detects intent, asks your custom qualification questions, and decides whether to transfer the call live, schedule a callback, book an appointment or send a WhatsApp follow-up. Every interaction is captured as structured data inside your CRM — turning your phone line into a true lead engagement software channel.

Why businesses choose FirstVoice

Traditional telephone answering services and live answering services are bound by human shifts, per-minute pricing and inconsistent quality. Legacy interactive voice response software frustrates callers with rigid menu trees. FirstVoice — as a modern AI receptionist software — eliminates both problems. It works as a true 24/7 call answering service, handles unlimited concurrent calls, and scales instantly during marketing campaigns or seasonal spikes without hiring or overtime cost.

FirstVoice as a complete business phone system

FirstVoice can operate as a standalone hosted phone system and cloud phone system on its own number, or sit on top of your existing business phone system / business telephone system as an AI answering and qualification layer. Combined with native call forwarding software, inbound call management software and call management software capabilities, it gives every team — sales, support, operations — a unified customer communication platform.

Built for sales and support automation

As customer support automation and customer interaction software, FirstVoice handles repetitive inbound queries — order status, business hours, pricing, FAQs — without tying up human agents. As a lead engagement software and voice automation software, it captures, qualifies and routes new leads in real time, with WhatsApp, email and CRM follow-ups firing automatically. The result is a measurable lift in conversion, response speed and customer satisfaction — at a fraction of the cost of a traditional virtual receptionist service or in-house team.

Enterprise security, multilingual coverage, fast deployment

FirstVoice is built with enterprise-grade encryption, role-based access and full audit trails. It supports multilingual voice AI across English, Hindi and major regional languages — making it the ideal phone answering software for Indian businesses serving diverse customer bases. Most customers go live within 48 hours, with full CRM, WhatsApp and calendar integration configured by the PixoraNest team.
